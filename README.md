# Bay Ready

A free tool for putting together an emergency kit if you live in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Answer a few quick questions about your household: how many people and pets, what you want to be ready for, and whether you want a bag you can grab, supplies to stay home, or both. You get a list that's sized for you.

## What you get

- A list of what to buy and pack, with the amounts worked out for your household
- A handful of free things you can knock out this weekend
- A spreadsheet you can download and share with the people you live with. It has a column for who's buying what, a spot for each person's go bag, and a place to keep track of when your food and water expire.

## Basics or Advanced

If you're new to this, start with Basics. It covers about a week at home plus a go bag. Advanced adds two weeks of supplies, a car kit, and some extra gear. You can switch back and forth without losing anything.

## Your info stays with you

There's no account and no sign-up. Your answers are saved in your browser. If you share your list, everything is in the link itself, so nothing gets sent to a server.

## Where the advice comes from

Bay Area counties, ReadySF, EBMUD, Cal OES, FEMA, the CDC, and the Red Cross. The Sources page links to all of them. Prices are rough guesses to help you plan, not quotes.

This isn't professional advice. In an emergency, call 911.
